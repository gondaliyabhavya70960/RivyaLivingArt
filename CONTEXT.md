## What we are building

Not a website — a two-sided system:

1. **A luxury public flagship showroom.** Cinematic, material-first, large-format-first. The visitor
   should feel as though they are approaching and understanding a physical object.
2. **An internal operating system (Rivya Studio).** Content, catalog, media, merchandising,
   inquiries, competitive research and operations — so the owner runs the business without a
   developer.

## The creative concept

```
WOOD → RESIN → LIGHT → FORM → SPACE → ART
```

The design question is never "how many components can we add?" — it is "how extraordinary can we
make the experience of discovering and understanding a Rivya object?" Every feature must serve
product understanding, material understanding, brand perception, storytelling, navigation,
conversion or usability. Anything else is removed.

The bar: *"I have never seen resin furniture presented like this."* Premium, artistic,
architectural, cinematic, tactile, calm, technically refined, trustworthy — with the technology
invisible behind the experience.

## Commercial model

Discovery and configuration happen on the site. Pricing, payment and delivery are agreed directly
with the owner over WhatsApp. The site's job is to make someone want to start that conversation,
and to capture enough structured detail that the conversation starts well.
